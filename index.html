About
Theresa W.
艺术家

"My creative spirit comes alive in the digital realm. With nimble fingers flying across the device, I sculpt light and matter into dialogue — each work a conversation between the physical and the infinite."

专注于雕塑、装置与数字媒介的跨界创作。作品探索材质、时间与空间的边界，以艺术哲学驱动视觉叙事。目前申请美国艺术类本科项目。
